About SAFISCO

Built on fabric that lasts and pricing that respects your budget

Who We Are

Your corporate t-shirt partner, from first sample to final delivery

SAFISCO manufactures and customizes corporate and team apparel for businesses across the UAE: polo shirts, crew neck tees, football and basketball jerseys, netball outfits and caps. Every order is finished in-house using embroidery, screen printing or full sublimation, so quality and turnaround stay in our control from start to finish.

Our garments are manufactured with care and shipped to businesses across the UAE, which lets us offer factory-level pricing without cutting corners on fabric weight, stitching or print durability.

Customization

Embroidery, screen printing and full sublimation, all in-house

Embroidery

Stitched logos for a premium, long-lasting finish: the standard choice for corporate polos, crew necks and caps. Embroidery holds up to years of washing without fading, cracking or peeling.

Best for: logos, wordmarks, small to medium designs on chest, sleeve or cap front.

Screen Printing

Bold, vibrant prints applied directly to the fabric, ideal for larger graphics, multi-color designs and event or promotional runs where cost-efficiency at volume matters.

Best for: front/back prints, event t-shirts, giveaways, bold brand graphics.

Full Sublimation

Dye is infused directly into the fabric fibers, so the design never cracks, peels or fades, even under intense training or match conditions. This is how we produce fully patterned football, basketball and netball kits with names, numbers and sponsor logos.

Best for: sports jerseys, netball outfits, all-over patterns and multi-color team kits.

Wide Range of High-Quality Cotton T-Shirts

We stock and manufacture cotton t-shirts across a broad range of colors and weights, so your order matches your brand exactly, no settling for whatever's in stock elsewhere.

Best for: staff uniforms, event giveaways and any order where color choice matters.

High-Quality Polo T-Shirts

Tailored-fit polos in durable pique fabric with clean, reinforced stitching, built to look sharp on day one and still hold their shape after months of wear and washing.

Best for: office uniforms, client-facing teams and staff branding.
